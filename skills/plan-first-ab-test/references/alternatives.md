# When another learning step is more useful

Choose a step that resolves the uncertainty in this particular decision. Low traffic is not a reason to stop learning, and missing traffic data is not evidence that traffic is low.

| What is uncertain?                                       | Useful next step                                                                                          | What it cannot establish                                                      |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Whether people understand an offer or instruction        | Watch relevant people attempt one realistic task; note what they misunderstand and where                  | Conversion lift for the full audience                                         |
| Why potential buyers reject the offer                    | Ask about recent actual attempts, alternatives and objections; inspect existing customer evidence         | A population-wide effect size or willingness to pay from hypothetical answers |
| Whether the conversion path works                        | Inspect the actual action, form validation, mobile behavior, errors and a staging completion              | The causal conversion effect of fixing it                                     |
| Whether recorded counts describe the intended population | Reconcile a small approved event trace and aggregate counts against the metric specification              | That every event in the warehouse is correct                                  |
| Where existing visitors appear to struggle               | Inspect relevant existing recordings, click/scroll evidence or support reports                            | Why behavior happened, or whether a proposed treatment will help              |
| Whether a small measured effect justifies waiting months | Revisit the decision deadline, worthwhile effect, implementation cost and alternative sources of evidence | Permission to inflate MDE or stop early for significance                      |

Do not install a heatmap provider by default, replace real users with invented AI personas, or label a before/after comparison a randomized test. A heuristic review should identify a few consequential issues and explain the proposed mechanism. Keep observed defects, interpretation and testable hypotheses separate.

Recommend one next action the user can actually take. For example: “Ask a relevant prospective customer to find the cancellation terms in this pricing page and describe what they expect to pay.” Record observations; do not turn a small qualitative exercise into a statistical win.
